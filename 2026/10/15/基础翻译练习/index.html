十月 15, 2026

基础翻译练习

原文：

I wake up at seven o’clock. I wash my face and brush my teeth. Then I eat breakfast. I like bread and milk.

I go to school at eight. My school is near my home. I walk to school with my friend. We talk and laugh.

In the evening, I do my homework. Then I watch TV with my family. I go to bed at ten.

译文：

我在七点起床，我洗了脸和刷了牙，然后我吃了早餐，我喜欢面包和牛奶。 我八点去了学校，我学校靠近我的家。我和我的朋友走去学校。我们很愉快。到了晚上，我完成我的作业。然后我看和我的家人看电视，在十点我去睡觉了。

I have a small family. There are four people in my family: my father, my mother, my sister, and me.

My father is a doctor. He works in a hospital. My mother is a teacher. She works in a school. My sister is ten years old. She likes to draw pictures.

We eat dinner together every day. I love my family.

我有一个小家，我的家庭有四口人。我的父亲，我的母亲，我的妹妹和我。我的父亲是个一声，他在医院工作。我的母亲是一个老师，她在学校工作。我的妹妹只有十岁，她喜欢画简笔画。我们每天都一起吃晚餐，我爱我的家庭。

I have a cat. Her name is Mimi. She is white and small. She has big blue eyes.

Mimi likes to play with a ball. She runs and jumps. She is very happy. She likes to sleep on my bed.

I give her food and water every day. She is my best friend.

我有一只猫。她的名字叫咪咪。她是一直小的白色的。她有一直蓝色的大眼睛。

咪咪喜欢玩球。她经常蹦蹦跳跳的。她非常开心。她喜欢在我的床上睡觉。我每天给她食物和水。她是我最好的朋友。

There is a park near my house. It is big and green. Many people go there.

Children play on the grass. Some people run. Some people walk their dogs. I like to sit under a tree and read a book.

The park is quiet in the morning. It is busy in the afternoon. I go there every weekend.

有个公园靠近我的家。是个大的绿色公园，很多人在这里。

小孩喜欢在草坪上玩。有时候人民会跑。有的人会和他们的狗会在这里散步。我喜欢坐在树下看书。这个公园在早晨十分安静。下午则很热闹，我每个周末都去这里。

Today is a rainy day. The sky is gray. I stay at home.

I look out the window. I see water on the glass. I hear the rain. It sounds nice.

I drink hot tea and read a book. My dog sleeps near me. I like rainy days.

今天是个雨天，天空是灰色的。我待在家里。

我透过窗户看向外面。我看到水顺着玻璃落下。我听到了余生。声音很好听。

我喝着热茶看着书。我的狗靠着我睡觉，我喜欢雨天。

Last Monday, Tom came home from school and wanted to open the door. He looked in his bag, but he could not find his key. He looked in his pockets. Nothing. He was worried because his parents were not at home.

Tom sat on the stairs and waited. After ten minutes, he felt cold and hungry. He decided to look again. This time, he opened every pocket slowly. Then he found the key. It was in a small pocket inside his jacket.

Tom laughed at himself. He opened the door, went inside, and made a hot sandwich. That day, he learned a lesson: when you cannot find something, look slowly and carefully.
